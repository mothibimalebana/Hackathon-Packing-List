# Hackathon-Packing-List
With the Telkom learn hackathon underway, I would like to share a list of things I usually pack with me to hackathons. Feel free to fork it or make a pull request to add your own items

# Hackathon Survival Kit 🧑‍💻  

Welcome to the **Hackathon Survival Kit**! Whether it’s your first overnight hackathon or your tenth, this list is here to help you stay prepared, productive, and (mostly) sane. Fork this repo or open a pull request to add your own essentials—we’re all about collaborative survival here.  

---

## 🎒 Packing List  

### 1. **Laptop + Charger**  
Because coding on vibes alone hasn't been ivented yet

### 2. **Headphones**  
For focus mode, team discussions, or drowning out chaos with lo-fi beats.  

### 3. **Power Bank**  
Because your phone at 2% isn’t as inspiring as it sounds when you’re debugging a live app.  

### 4. **Snacks**  
Debugging is easier when you’re not hungry.  
*Pro Tip*: Avoid snacks that crumble into your keyboard.  

### 5. **Water Bottle**  
Caffeine is great, but water keeps your brain firing on all cylinders.  

### 6. **Comfy Clothes**  
Hoodies, sweatpants, and sneakers—your go-to uniform for conquering code and cold venues.  

### 7. **Notebook + Pen**  
Perfect for sketching ideas, jotting down eureka moments, or stress-doodling.  

### 8. **Toiletries**  
Pack the basics: toothbrush, deodorant, face wipes—you’ll thank yourself later.  

### 9. **Backup Wi-Fi**  
Portable router or hotspot? Yes, please. The team with stable internet wins the race.  

### 10. **Positive Energy**  
Bring that can-do attitude! Overnight hackathons are all about the journey (and hopefully fewer merge conflicts).
